Quick test to identify the bus (multimeter on DC volts):

Identify power pins
One pin will be +V (often ~24V or ~5V depending on system).
One pin will be GND (0V).
Measure the two remaining pins (data lines):
CAN bus: both sit around ~2.5V at idle and move together (one rises while the other falls when active).
UART: one pin will toggle 0–5V or 0–3.3V. The other may be unused or steady.
Analog joystick: the two lines will be steady voltages that shift smoothly when you move the joystick (often ~0.5–4.5V).

To unlock a  Jazzy Elite power chair, you must perform a specific sequence with the joystick or horn button, depending on which controller model you have. 
Method 1: Horn Button (Standard for Most Elite Models)
If your battery lights are scrolling back and forth or flashing, the chair is likely in "Lock Mode". 

    Turn on the power.
    Press the horn button twice within 10 seconds of the lights starting to scroll.
    The lights should become steady, indicating the chair is unlocked. 

Method 2: Joystick Sequence (GC or VR2 Controllers)
If the horn method doesn't work, try this common alternative sequence:

    Turn on the controller.
    Push the joystick full forward until you hear a beep.
    Pull the joystick full backward until you hear another beep.
    Release the joystick to its central position. You should hear one long beep confirming it is unlocked. 

Additional Troubleshooting

    Freewheel Levers: If the chair powers on but won't move (and isn't beeping/scrolling), ensure the manual freewheel levers are in the "Drive" (locked) position. For most Elite models, rotate or pull the levers toward the seat to engage the motors.
    Check Connections: If the chair still won't respond, ensure the main controller cable is securely plugged into the power base.
    Battery Voltage: A single flashing red LED typically means the battery is too low to operate and needs immediate charging.
